# Consistency fixes

Changes made from the consistency read of chapters 1 to 22.

- Signpost ("A grave says three things."): deleted; the paragraph now opens "First, a grave says that a body is not rubbish".
- Repeated tic ("In other words", twice): changed the graveside one to "The people at that graveside, then, shared"; kept the one on believing bands.
- Thread assumed (San and Ju/'hoansi read as two groups): "The Ju/'hoansi of the Kalahari show how it works" became "The Ju/'hoansi, one of the San peoples, show how it works."
- Repeated tic ("Kalahari"): "as beads still move in the Kalahari" became "as beads still move among the Ju/'hoansi".
