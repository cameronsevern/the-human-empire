# Lookups: Chapter 28, How did Islam begin?

Web fact check, with a second reading for how Muslim belief and the tradition are reported (Sunni and Shia). Primary sources consulted through summaries and translations: Ibn Ishaq/Ibn Hisham (Guillaume), Sahih al-Bukhari 3 and 3668, the Qur'anic verses named, the Constitution of Medina clauses, the Doctrina Jacobi and Sebeos in standard editions as quoted. Verdicts: correct / imprecise / wrong / could not verify. Chapter was not edited.
