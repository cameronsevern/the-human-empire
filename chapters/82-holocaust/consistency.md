# Consistency fixes

- Contradiction (81: three million Soviet prisoners by December 1941, two million dead by spring; 82: three million prisoners of war let die): 82 now gives the war-long totals, nearly six million taken and more than three million dead, so the two figures no longer read as the same group. Source: https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/nazi-persecution-of-soviet-prisoners-of-war (5.7 million taken, 3.3 million dead).
