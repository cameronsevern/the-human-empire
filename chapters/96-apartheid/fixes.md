- Part A MINOR, "South Africa was the one place on the continent where Europeans had settled in large numbers and stayed for centuries": fixed (Portuguese settlement in Angola and Mozambique went back to the 1500s, though it stayed small). "No other country in Africa had so many European settlers, and many of their families had been there for two centuries or more." South Africa had about 3.1 million white people in 1960, by far the largest white population in Africa; Algeria's settlers numbered about one million.
