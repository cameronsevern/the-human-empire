- Contradiction (Judah's exiles stated flatly here; 14 says the clay cylinder does not mention Judah and gives the return on the Bible's word): cut "among them the people of Judah", so 14 carries the Judah story.
- Contradiction (the object has three names: "barrel-shaped lump of baked clay" in the body, "clay cylinder" in the caption, "clay barrel" in 14): body now "a cylinder of baked clay, shaped like a barrel", matching the caption and the museum name. 14 was changed to "clay cylinder" to match.
- Voice drift (source criticism; the Bardiya impostor argued over three sentences): cut to one, "Most historians today think it more likely that Darius and his friends killed the true Bardiya and then invented the impostor."
- Retold story (the alphabet explained five times): "the twenty-two letters that the Assyrian scribes with their scrolls and pens had already used" became "the short alphabet that the Assyrian scribes had already used".
