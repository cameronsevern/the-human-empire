# Fixes from the flow-pass fix list

- Part A MAJOR, Arkhipov scene (his argument causing the surfacing): fixed by rebuilding the sequence. B-59 surfaced that night to recharge; on the surface, low passes and cannon fire made the captain order a dive and the nuclear torpedo readied; Arkhipov, still on the tower, saw the Americans were signalling and talked him down. New passage: "...In the end, however, the batteries decided what happened next. That night B-59 had to come up to recharge them, into the glare of American searchlights. / On the surface, things got worse. American planes roared low over the conning tower, the tall structure on top of the hull where the officers stood, firing their cannon. The captain, half blinded, took it for an attack. He ordered an emergency dive and called for the nuclear torpedo to be made ready. / ... Arkhipov was still standing on the tower, and he could see what the captain had missed: the planes were firing past the boat, not at it. The Americans were signalling, not attacking. He called the captain back and talked him down. / The order never reached the torpedo room. The next day B-59, her batteries full, slipped back under the sea and away from her pursuers. ..." The underwater "We will blow them up now" quotation (Orlov's disputed account) and the consent claim were cut; "the man who had refused to fire" became "the man who had stopped the launch" and "that afternoon" became "that night" later in the chapter.
