# Consistency fixes

His reign is usually put at about 2378 to 2370 BCE. Sources: https://cdli.ox.ac.uk/wiki/urukagina and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urukagina
- Ending pointed at the next subject ("What remained to be settled was who would command the men..."): now ends on an image from inside the chapter, the same clerks measuring out barley for spearmen and for weavers in the same columns.
