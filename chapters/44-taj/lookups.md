## Fixes in short

1. Rows 5 and 6: replace the week and the beard with a year of mourning and white hair.
2. Row 21: marble from Makrana lay in Marwar; the raja was ordered to supply stonecutters and carts.
3. Row 13: jade from China.
4. Row 27: Babur's own stated reason was Kabul's poverty.
5. Rows 44 and 45: soften the "two centuries" and drop "around 1516".
6. Row 83: reason for more Hindu officers was Maratha recruitment.
7. Row 91: about two kilometres.
