# Fixes from the flow-pass re-check

- Part B CONFLICT, Missouri: correct as written (new wording kept). "In 1820 it had agreed to admit Missouri as a slave state..." The compromise was passed in 1820; Missouri was admitted in August 1821, so the old "had admitted Missouri" in 1820 was wrong. Source: https://www.loc.gov/rr/program/bib/ourdocs/missouri.html
- Part B CONFLICT, railway companies: correct as written (new wording kept). "...Congress authorised two companies to build one..." The 1862 act chartered the Union Pacific but the Central Pacific already held a California charter of 1861, so "authorised" is right and "chartered two companies" was not. Source: https://american-rails.com/cprr.html (Central Pacific incorporated 28 June 1861)
