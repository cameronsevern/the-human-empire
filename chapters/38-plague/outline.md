# Chapter 38 — What did the Black Death change?

**Line under the title:** Central Asia, Europe and Egypt, from 1338 to about 1500.
**Length:** 4,000 to 4,800 words.
**Voice:** `style.md`. Answer the question early. No hedging; replace weak examples. Lead with transitions. Give the reader room. Plain numbered years.

## The idea the chapter leaves the reader with

The plague killed close to half the people between Central Asia and the Atlantic in about five years, and it kept coming back for three centuries. Death on that scale made working hands scarce, and what scarcity did depended on who had the power to bargain. In England, lords competed with each other for tenants and labourers, the law that tried to freeze wages failed, and within a century and a half serfdom had faded away and ordinary people ate and dressed better than their grandparents. In Egypt the land was watered by a huge system of dikes and canals that needed thousands of hands every year, and it was held by soldier-landlords who lived in Cairo and squeezed harder as the villages emptied. There the dikes failed, the fields went out of use, and the country grew poorer. The same disease produced opposite results.

## The story

1. **Opening: the stones by the lake.** Answer first. Gravestones of 1338 and 1339 beside a lake in Kyrgyzstan, and what was found in the teeth of the people under them.
2. **Twelve galleys.** Fleas, rats and ships. From the Black Sea to Sicily in 1347, and around Europe and the Middle East in five years. What the disease does.
3. **Like lasagne.** Florence and Siena. What people thought was happening: the stars, bad air, the wrath of God. Whippers and the murder of the Jews. The forty days.
4. **Eight pence and his dinner.** England before and after. The law that fixed wages, and why lords broke it.
5. **When Adam delved.** The rising of 1381 and its failure. Serfdom fades anyway. Meat, ale and sheep.
6. **The dikes of the Nile.** How Egypt was watered, who owned it, and what happened when the diggers died.
7. **Who could walk away.** Why the difference.
8. **Ending: the unfinished wall.**
