## Factual flags

1. **Minor — a seasonal peak becomes an unqualified concentration.**
   
   > “Today it is above four hundred and thirty.”
   
   For the chapter’s 2025 endpoint, this is misleading without a place and season. Mauna Loa’s May 2025 average exceeded 430 ppm; its **annual average was 427.35 ppm**. The correct claim is that the seasonal peak at Mauna Loa passed 430, not that the general atmospheric concentration stood above it throughout 2025. [Scripps announcement](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/annual-carbon-dioxide-peak-passes-another-milestone), [NOAA annual measurements](https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_annmean_mlo.txt).

2. **Minor — “first clue” claims too much historical priority.**
   
   > “The first clue came from a simple experiment.”
   
   Read as the first clue to atmospheric warming, this overlooks Fourier’s explanation of the atmosphere’s warming role in 1824. Foote’s 1856 experiment was an early demonstration concerning particular gases, not the first clue to the greenhouse effect generally. Specify that narrower contribution or remove “first.” [IPCC historical account](https://archive.ipcc.ch/publications_and_data/ar4/wg1/en/ch1s1-4.html).

3. **Minor — clean background air is confused with air untouched by sources.**
   
   > “On Mauna Loa, by contrast, the instrument could sample air that had been nowhere near either.”
   
   The measurement does not establish that the air has never passed a chimney or forest. It samples well-mixed background air with little recent local influence; locally affected readings require screening. Mixing preserves the accumulated signal from distant sources, which is precisely why the record is useful. [NOAA’s measurement explanation](https://gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/about/co2_measurements.pdf).

4. **Minor — air bubbles are assigned the snowfall’s date.**
   
   > “Each dark spot is a bubble of air that was sealed in when the snow fell.”
   
   Air is sealed in **later**, when burial and compaction close the pores. It can be substantially younger than the surrounding ice, sometimes by thousands of years. The caption should say that bubbles were trapped as snow compacted into ice. [MIT explanation of ice-core dating](https://climate.mit.edu/ask-mit/when-scientists-reconstruct-ancient-climates-ice-cores-how-do-they-create-accurate-timeline).

5. **Minor — the ice-core record needs a defined scope.**
   
   > “The deepest bubbles are eight hundred thousand years old.”
   
   Eight hundred thousand years describes the established continuous record being discussed, not the oldest recovered trapped air. By October 2025, researchers had reported discontinuous ice-and-air samples dating to six million years. Identify the continuous record rather than presenting 800,000 years as a general limit. [WHOI’s research announcement](https://www.whoi.edu/press-room/news-release/six-million-year-old-ice-discovered-in-antarctica-offers-unprecedented-window-into-a-warmer-earth/).

6. **Major — the local-versus-global contrast rests on a false boundary.**
   
   > “That was the easy part, since the pollution and its victims were in the same country.”
   
   The air and water pollution discussed was not confined to national borders. Acid rain was a major contemporary counterexample: emissions crossed from the United States into Canada and across European borders. The defensible distinction is that many conventional pollution controls deliver more immediate local or regional benefits; their victims were not necessarily in the emitting country. [Canadian government history of acid rain](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/air-pollution/issues/acid-rain-causes-effects/history.html).

7. **Minor — Copenhagen was not the next attempt after Kyoto.**
   
   > “The next attempt went worse.”
   
   Negotiations continued between 1997 and 2009. The Montreal process began in 2005, and the 2007 Bali Road Map explicitly organised negotiations towards Copenhagen. “Next” falsely compresses that chronology. Copenhagen can be introduced directly without listing the intervening meetings. [UNFCCC account of the Bali Road Map](https://unfccc.int/process/conferences/the-big-picture/milestones/bali-road-map).
