# Chapter 104 — Why did Russia invade Ukraine?

**Line under the title:** Ukraine and Russia, from 1991 to 2025.
**Length:** 3,800 to 4,600 words.
**Voice:** `style.md`. Answer the question early. No hedging; replace weak examples. Lead with transitions. Give the reader room. Plain numbered years. The causes are disputed; set out the competing accounts plainly, and say what the evidence supports. The war was not over at the end of 2025: write the ending so that it stays true.

## The idea the chapter leaves the reader with

Russia invaded because its ruler does not accept that Ukraine is a separate nation, and because he feared that he was about to lose it for good to the democratic and prosperous West. He has said that the cause was the eastward growth of the American alliance, and some Western scholars agree that the alliance's expansion was reckless. But the alliance had offered Ukraine nothing concrete, and his own words, in an essay written seven months before the attack, describe a country that has no right to exist apart from Russia. He expected Kyiv to fall in days. It did not, because Ukrainians, including those who speak Russian, turned out to believe in their country. The war that followed is the largest in Europe since 1945, and it has been fought with a mixture of trenches and cheap flying robots that nobody had seen before.
