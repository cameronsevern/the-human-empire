# Chapter 112 — What did the pandemic show us?

**Line under the title:** The world, from 2019 to 2023.
**Length:** 3,800 to 4,600 words.
**Voice:** `style.md`. Answer the question early. No hedging; replace weak examples. Lead with transitions. Give the reader room. Plain numbered years. Recent and contested: give figures with their source, and state what is not known as not known.

## The idea the chapter leaves the reader with

It showed how tightly the world is now joined, and what each society really protects. A virus that appeared in one Chinese city was on every continent within weeks, carried by air travel. The same connections let scientists who had never met design a vaccine within days of the virus's code being published, and test and make it within a year, which had never been done. Between those two facts some twenty million people died. Who they were depended on age above all, then on poverty and on the work they did, and on something that surprised the experts: whether people trusted their government and one another mattered more than how rich or well-equipped a country was. The vaccine was shared as unequally as everything else. Earlier plagues left behind quarantine, sewers and public health. It is too soon to say what this one has left.
