# Fixes from the flow-pass re-check

- Part A MINOR, emancipation timing reads as 1837: fixed. "In 1833 it voted to end slavery itself in the Caribbean colonies. The law took effect the next year, and after four more years of compulsory unpaid work for their old masters, the people were free in 1838." Source: https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/tradeindustry/slavetrade/overview/the-west-indian-colonies-and-emancipation/
- Part A MINOR, 1805 constitution exception for "a few hundred Poles and Germans who had deserted": fixed. "His constitution declared that no white man could own land in Haiti, with exceptions for white women who had become Haitian, their children, and the Germans and Poles whom the government had made citizens." (Articles 12 to 14.) Source: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Hayti_(1805)
- Part A MINOR, free people of colour owned "about a third of the colony's plantations and a quarter of its slaves": fixed. "Free people of colour owned plantations of their own, many of them owned slaves, and they supplied most of the militia that hunted runaways." Source: John Garrigus, Before Haiti, https://link.springer.com/book/9781403971401 (the one-third figure was a contemporary claim, not a count)
