# Chapter 10 — Why did it take so long to invent the wheel?

**Line under the title:** Europe, the Middle East and the grasslands of Asia, from about 5,500 years ago to about 3,600 years ago.
**Length:** 3,500 to 4,500 words.
**Voice:** `style.md`. Answer the question early. No hedging; replace weak examples. Lead with transitions. Give the reader room.

## The idea the chapter leaves the reader with

The wheel is hard to make and useless without three other things: metal tools to cut an exact fit between wheel and axle, a strong animal to pull, and ground worth rolling on. All three came together about 5,500 years ago, and within a few centuries carts were in use from the North Sea to Iraq. The Americas had the idea and no animal. The wagon then opened the one great landscape that farmers and hunters had both left nearly empty, the grasslands, because a family could now carry its house and its water and follow its herds. The people who did so tamed the horse, built the first light chariot, and spread so widely that almost half the people alive today speak a language descended from theirs.

## The story

1. **A wheel in a bog.** Answer first. The wooden wheel from the marshes of Slovenia, and why it looks so clumsy.
2. **The axle is the hard part.** Rollers are not wheels. What has to fit, and how exactly. Copper chisels. Why the potter's wheel is a different machine.
3. **Everywhere at once.** The pot from Poland, the ruts under a Danish-German grave mound, the signs from Uruk. One invention, spread in a few centuries. Oxen.
4. **Toys in Mexico.** The Americas had the wheel as an idea and nothing to pull it.
5. **The sea of grass.** Why the steppe was nearly empty. The wagon as a house on wheels. Graves with wagons in them.
6. **The horse.** Hunted, then herded, then ridden. The breed that replaced all others about 4,200 years ago.
7. **The language in the cart.** Words for wheel and axle shared from Ireland to India. The bones agree.
8. **Spokes.** The chariot graves east of the Urals. A weapon arrives in the old kingdoms.
