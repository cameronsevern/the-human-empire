## Not found / weak

- Early Mesopotamian clay model wagon or solid tripartite wheel: nothing usable located; the Standard of Ur stands in for it.
- Museum reconstruction of a Yamnaya or Catacomb wagon: only a grave plan (CC BY) and a 270 px drawing were found; no photograph of a full-size reconstruction.
- Sintashta chariot burial plan or full chariot reconstruction: not found. Only the cheekpiece/pottery drawing and the Arkaim aerial.
- Kazakh steppe landscape: "The great Eurasian steppe.jpg" proved to be a Kyiv city square, so it was rejected. The Mongolian steppe photo is the substitute. A Kazakh or Pontic landscape without wires, and a stand-alone kurgan photograph, were not located.
- A labelled map of the steppe belt: the steppe-belt file has no labels; Indo-European migrations map has English labels but also shows many later movements.
- The Bronocice pot photograph may show the pot as reconstructed in a display; check the museum label before captioning with a precise date.
