# Chapter 100 — Why did the Soviet Union collapse?

**Line under the title:** The Soviet Union and eastern Europe, from 1979 to 1999.
**Length:** 3,800 to 4,600 words.
**Voice:** `style.md`. Answer the question early. No hedging; replace weak examples. Lead with transitions. Give the reader room. Plain numbered years.

## The idea the chapter leaves the reader with

The Soviet Union collapsed because its planned economy could no longer feed its people decently or keep pace with its rival, and because the man who set out to repair it removed the two things that held it together, which were fear and silence. Once people were allowed to speak, they said what they thought of the system, and the nations that had been held inside the empire asked to leave. He would not shoot them to keep them. Eastern Europe went in six months in 1989, and the Union itself two years later, with very little bloodshed, which has few parallels in the history of empires. What followed in Russia was a decade of poverty and theft, and out of that came a ruler who called the collapse a catastrophe.
