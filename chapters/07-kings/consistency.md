# Consistency fixes

Changes made from the consistency read of chapters 1 to 22.

- Signpost ("There are two reasons, and neither requires anyone to have been especially wicked."): now "Nobody had to be especially wicked for them to stay.", and "The first is that" became "For one thing,".
- Repeated tic ("It appears, in other words, in one place after another"): dropped "in other words".
- Ending pointed at the next subject (the cupbearer of Kish "preparing to try"): deleted that last sentence, so the chapter ends on the need for "a way of being obeyed in places where he was not standing". The cupbearer is introduced in full at the start of 08, so nothing is lost. This also removes the third date in the 6/7/8 chronology finding; the remaining dates (06 at 4,400, 08 at 4,350) now run in order.
