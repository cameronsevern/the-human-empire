# Chapter 94 — Why did America lose the war in Vietnam?

**Line under the title:** Vietnam, from 1945 to 1975.
**Length:** 3,800 to 4,600 words.
**Voice:** `style.md`. Answer the question early. No hedging; replace weak examples. Lead with transitions. Give the reader room. Plain numbered years.

## The idea the chapter leaves the reader with

America lost because it was fighting a different war from its enemy. To Washington, Vietnam was a square on the board of the Cold War, where communism had to be stopped. To the Vietnamese who fought on the other side, it was the last stage of a struggle to rid their country of foreign rule, which had begun against the French and for which they were prepared to go on dying without limit. The United States could destroy anything it could find, and it dropped more bombs than in the whole of the Second World War, but it could not make the people of the South believe in the governments it propped up. In the end the American public, which watched the war on television and supplied its conscripts, decided that it was not worth the cost.
