## Summary

Fetched pages or search results support the ceremony details, the partition figures, Deir Yassin, Haifa, the Hadassah convoy, Exodus 1947, the King David Hotel, the 1948 casualty figure, the Iraq laws and the refugee numbers. The dates and numbers for Herzl, the British limit, the Jerusalem siege, the Czech arms and Egyptian motives rest on own knowledge. Items 11 (diary phrase), 5 (blackout) and 3 (chairs) could not be found in any source.
