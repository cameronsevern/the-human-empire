# Consistency read: changes

- Repeated "Consider what..." opener (also used in chapter 40): "Consider what it meant for one of them. The journey" changed to "For one of those men, the journey".
- Teaser signpost ("it was nothing beside what came next"): cut the tease; the next sentence now carries the point on its own.
- "therefore" thinned (7 to 4): dropped it from "It was therefore a young empire", "Almost everything the town needed therefore had to be brought in" and "Whole families therefore took the road" (which already followed a "because").
