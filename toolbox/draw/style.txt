House style, identical for every picture in this series: a hand-inked technical illustration in the manner of a museum field guide, fine dark-brown ink linework with soft watercolour washes, on warm cream paper with a thin ink border. Muted earth palette (bone, tan, clay, stone grey). One accent colour, terracotta red, is used only on the part that makes the tool work. Water, where it appears, is a pale blue-grey. Landscape format, 3:2. Title in the top left in serif capitals with the date beneath it in small italics. Numbered callouts: a small dot on the part, a thin leader line, a ringed number, and a short serif label. Spell every label exactly as given and add no other text. No people's faces in detail, no modern objects, no decorative flourishes.
