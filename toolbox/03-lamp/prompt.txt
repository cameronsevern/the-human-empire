Title: THE LAMP. Date line: about 30,000 years ago. An ice-age lamp: a slab of reddish sandstone about as long as a hand, with a shallow round hollow ground into one end and a short handle at the other, like a stone spoon. The hollow holds pale melted animal fat. A small wick lies in the fat with one end raised, and a small steady yellow flame burns at its tip. The wick is the only terracotta in the picture. Dark background is NOT wanted: keep the cream paper. Place the labels in clear space so that no label overlaps the lamp or another label. Callouts: 1 'A hollow ground into a slab of stone'; 2 'Animal fat. It melts as the stone warms'; 3 'A wick of moss or a juniper twig'; 4 'A steady flame, about as bright as a candle'; 5 'A handle that stays cool'.
