## Why it mattered

Fosters of Lincoln built heavy tractors for hauling guns, and the tank took the tractor's 105-horsepower Daimler engine to move 28 tons. Its top speed was about six kilometres an hour, a brisk walk. The tracks ran right round the hull, so that the high front could climb out of a trench and the long body could bridge one.

Inside, eight men shared the space with the engine. The air could reach 50 degrees, thick with petrol and cordite fumes, and it was so loud that the driver got the gearsmen's attention by striking the engine with a spanner. Messages to the rear went by carrier pigeon. Of 150 Mark Is built, one survives, at the Tank Museum in Bovington, Dorset.
