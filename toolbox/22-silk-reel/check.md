1. **Overbroad account of silk-making reaching the Mediterranean** — [line 31](toolbox/22-silk-reel/profile.md:31)

   > “Silk-making reached the Mediterranean about 1,500 years ago, and the reel went with it.”

   That date fits the introduction of **domesticated mulberry silkworm cultivation into Byzantium**, not Mediterranean silk-making generally. Mediterranean weavers already worked imported silk, and ancient accounts describe wild-silk production on Cos. Name the particular technology that arrived. [Oxford Classical Dictionary, “silk”](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/550500768).

2. **Reeled silk presented as all silk** — [lines 23 and 31](toolbox/22-silk-reel/profile.md:23)

   > “Silk needs no spinning, because the caterpillar has already made a fibre a kilometre long.”

   > “The thread in a silk scarf or tie was drawn off several cocoons at once and stuck together with the caterpillar's own gum.”

   These describe **reeled filament silk**, but silk yarn is also spun from short fibres recovered from damaged cocoons and processing waste. Consequently, an unspecified silk garment need not have been made by the illustrated process. Narrow both statements to reeled silk. This is also a concrete exception to the style rule against exception-breaking absolutes. [FAO, §8.5, “Spun Silk”](https://www.fao.org/4/x2099E/x2099e09.htm).

3. **Incomplete explanation of strength** — [line 23](toolbox/22-silk-reel/profile.md:23)

   > “The reel only has to get it off the cocoon in one piece, and that is why silk cloth is so smooth and so strong for its weight.”

   Keeping filaments long helps yarn strength and smoothness, but does not by itself explain silk’s strength for its weight. That also depends on the structure of the silk protein; fabric construction and processing matter too. Separate the benefit of continuous filaments from the fibre’s inherent strength. [Research on silk fibroin’s structure and strength](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4032545/).

4. **Step 2 changes the label’s claim** — [line 16](toolbox/22-silk-reel/profile.md:16)
