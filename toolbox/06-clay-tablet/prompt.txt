Title: THE CLAY TABLET. Date line: about 5,300 years ago. Three stages in a row from left to right, joined by two arrows. Stage one: a few small clay tokens, cones and balls. Stage two: a hollow ball of clay with a wedge cut away to show tokens inside, and the outside covered with small pressed marks (circles and triangles) outlined in terracotta. Stage three: a small flat pillow-shaped clay tablet divided into four boxes, with pressed number marks in terracotta and two simple scratched drawings (an ear of barley and a jar), with a cut reed lying below it. Callouts: 1 'Tokens. A cone is a small measure of grain, a ball a large one'; 2 'The tokens are sealed inside a hollow ball of clay'; 3 'Each token is pressed into the outside first, so the count can be read'; 4 'With the marks outside, the ball is not needed. A flat pad will do'; 5 'A drawing, scratched with a reed, says what was counted'.
