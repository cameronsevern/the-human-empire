Title: THE CLAY ENVELOPE. Date line: about 4,000 years ago. Three stages in a row from left to right, joined by two small arrows, showing how an ancient Mesopotamian contract was sealed. Stage one, at the left: a small pillow-shaped clay tablet covered in rows of fine wedge-shaped cuneiform writing. Stage two, in the middle: the same tablet half enclosed in a thin outer shell of clay, with part of the shell cut away so the written tablet can be seen inside it. Stage three, at the right: the finished closed clay envelope, slightly larger than the tablet, with cuneiform writing on its outer face and a band across its middle printed with a row of small figures from a rolled cylinder seal; that printed band is the only terracotta-accented part of the picture. All the clay is a warm pale brown. Place the labels in clear space so that no label overlaps an object or another label. Callouts: 1 'The agreement is written on a tablet'; 2 'A thin sheet of clay is wrapped round it and pinched shut'; 3 'The same words are written again on the outside'; 4 'The parties and witnesses roll their seals across it'; 5 'If the two ever disagree, a judge breaks the envelope and reads the tablet inside'.
