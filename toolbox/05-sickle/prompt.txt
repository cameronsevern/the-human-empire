Title: THE SICKLE. Date line: about 15,000 years ago. A Stone Age harvesting sickle: a gently curved handle of wood with a row of eight small flint blades set into a groove along the inner edge, the glue line in terracotta. A circular inset shows a cut through the handle with a flint blade glued into the groove, captioned 'Cut through the handle'. A few stalks of WILD wheat stand inside the curve: thin, sparse ears with only a few slender grains each and long fine bristles, on spindly stems, clearly not plump modern farm wheat. Callouts: 1 'Handle of wood or bone'; 2 'Blades of flint, each as long as a finger joint'; 3 'Tree resin glues the blades into a groove'; 4 'The curve gathers a bunch of stalks, and one pull cuts them'.
