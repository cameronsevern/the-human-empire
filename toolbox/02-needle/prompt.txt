Title: THE NEEDLE. Date line: more than 40,000 years ago. An ice-age sewing needle of polished bone, long and slender, lying diagonally, with a small oval eye at the thick end ringed in terracotta and a thread of sinew passing through it. A circular inset shows two pieces of animal hide joined by a neat row of stitches in terracotta, captioned 'A sewn seam'. Callouts: 1 'A splinter of bone, ground smooth on a stone'; 2 'The eye, bored with a point of flint'; 3 'Thread of animal sinew'; 4 'Stitches pull two skins tight, so no wind gets through'.
