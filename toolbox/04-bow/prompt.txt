Title: THE BOW. Date line: more than 60,000 years ago. An archaeology museum diagram explaining how a prehistoric wooden archery bow works as a spring. No people, no animals, no target: only the object. A plain one-piece wooden stave bent into a curve at the right, a string drawn back to a point at the left, and one arrow resting across the middle pointing right. The arrow has a wooden shaft, feathers at the back and a small chipped-stone tip bound on with cord. A faint dashed line shows where the string rests when the bow is relaxed.
