## Why it mattered

A piston engine strong enough to lift a helicopter full of men was itself heavy, and it left little to spare for the load. The Huey's turbine gave far more power for its weight. That made the helicopter an everyday workhorse of the army, and more than 7,000 Hueys served in Vietnam.

It also changed what a wound meant. Ambulance Hueys, known by their radio call sign, Dustoff, flew a medic to the injured and brought most of them to a surgeon in under an hour. In Korea the same journey might take several hours. The cost was high: more than 3,300 Hueys were destroyed, and nearly 2,400 of their pilots and crew were killed.
